# Synapse

## Enterprise Intelligence Platform

**Author:** Sarath Warrier
**Created:** 23 August 2026  
**Status:** Initial Definition

Synapse is an enterprise intelligence platform that transforms
business data into a unified understanding of an organization's
operations, performance, and opportunities.

Synapse brings together information from disparate business systems,
establishes a common understanding of the enterprise, and applies
business intelligence to help people understand what is happening,
why it is happening, and what deserves attention.

---

## Purpose

Organizations operate across countries, companies, departments, and
systems. Information is often fragmented across these boundaries,
making it difficult to obtain a coherent view of the business.

Synapse exists to provide that view.

It connects business information across the organization and turns
fragmented data into a consistent, contextual, and actionable picture
of the enterprise.

---

## What Synapse Provides

### Unified Business View

A single view of the organization across:

- Companies
- Countries
- Business units
- Customers
- Suppliers
- Products
- Transactions
- Financials
- Operations

### Business Intelligence

Synapse helps users understand:

- Performance
- Trends
- Variances
- Relationships
- Drivers
- Risks
- Opportunities
- Forecasts

### Cross-Enterprise Intelligence

Business activity is not isolated to individual departments,
companies, or systems.

Synapse is designed to identify relationships across the enterprise,
including:

- Cross-company activity
- Intercompany relationships
- Supply and demand relationships
- Customer and supplier relationships
- Financial and operational relationships

### Decision Support

Synapse should move beyond presenting information.

It should help answer:

> What happened?

> Why did it happen?

> What is likely to happen?

> What requires attention?

> What could we do about it?

---

## Product Principles

### One Enterprise

Synapse represents the organization as a connected enterprise rather
than a collection of isolated systems or departments.

### Business First

The business meaning of information is more important than the
technical structure of its source.

### Intelligence Over Reporting

Reporting tells users what the data says.

Synapse should help users understand what the data means.

### Context Matters

Numbers without context are insufficient.

Synapse should consider relationships, history, organizational
structure, and business context when generating insights.

### Source Agnostic

The intelligence provided by Synapse should not depend on the system
from which the underlying information originated.

### Explainable

Important insights should be understandable and traceable back to the
underlying business information and reasoning.

### Extensible

Synapse should be capable of expanding with the organization as new
business areas, data sources, and intelligence capabilities are
introduced.

---

## The Intelligence Loop

Synapse follows a simple conceptual progression:

    Business Data
         ↓
    Business Context
         ↓
    Understanding
         ↓
    Intelligence
         ↓
    Decision Support

The objective is not merely to collect more data.

The objective is to create better understanding.

---

## Core Capabilities

Synapse is intended to evolve across capabilities such as:

- Enterprise analytics
- KPI management
- Performance analysis
- Trend analysis
- Variance analysis
- Forecasting
- Anomaly detection
- Risk identification
- Opportunity identification
- Cross-enterprise analysis
- Natural language business intelligence
- Recommendations and decision support

Capabilities should be introduced according to business value rather
than technology availability.
